COMPANY POLICIES

1. Working Hours
   - Standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
   - Employees are expected to maintain punctuality and inform their manager in case of delays.

2. Code of Conduct
   - Employees must maintain professionalism and respect towards colleagues, clients, and management.
   - Harassment, discrimination, or any form of misconduct will not be tolerated.

3. Dress Code
   - Business casual attire is required on regular working days.
   - Formal attire is expected during client meetings and official events.

4. Confidentiality
   - Employees must protect company data, client information, and trade secrets.
   - Sharing confidential information with unauthorized parties is strictly prohibited.

5. Attendance
   - Employees must record attendance daily through the company portal.
   - Frequent unexplained absences may lead to disciplinary action.


LEAVE POLICIES

1. Casual Leave (CL)
   - 12 days per year.
   - Must be applied for at least 1 day in advance, except in emergencies.

2. Sick Leave (SL)
   - 10 days per year.
   - Medical certificate required for leaves exceeding 3 consecutive days.

3. Earned/Privilege Leave (EL/PL)
   - 15 days per year, accrued monthly.
   - Can be carried forward up to 30 days; excess is encashed at year-end.

4. Maternity Leave
   - 26 weeks as per statutory guidelines.

5. Paternity Leave
   - 10 working days.

6. Public Holidays
   - As per the company's annual holiday calendar (typically 10-12 declared holidays).

7. Leave Application Process
   - All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal and approved by the reporting manager.
