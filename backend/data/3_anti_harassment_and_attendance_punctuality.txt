ANTI-HARASSMENT AND ANTI-BULLYING POLICY

Company: Hilip Tech
Effective Date: 9/9/2026

PURPOSE
-------
The Company is committed to maintaining a workplace free from harassment, intimidation, and bullying of any kind.


DEFINITION
----------
Harassment includes any unwelcome conduct based on a protected characteristic that creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive work environment. This includes verbal, physical, and visual conduct, as well as sexual harassment.

  - Unwelcome remarks, jokes, or slurs relating to a protected characteristic.
  - Unwanted physical contact or sexual advances.
  - Repeated belittling, intimidating, or aggressive behavior (bullying).
  - Display or circulation of offensive material, including digitally.


REPORTING AND INVESTIGATION
---------------------------
Any employee who experiences or witnesses harassment or bullying should report it to their manager or HR without fear of retaliation. All complaints will be investigated promptly, fairly, and confidentially to the extent possible.


CONSEQUENCES
------------
Substantiated violations of this policy will result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

ATTENDANCE AND PUNCTUALITY POLICY


Company: Hilip Tech
Effective Date: 9/9/2026

PURPOSE
-------
Reliable attendance and punctuality are essential to maintaining productivity and team coordination.


POLICY
------
  - Employees are expected to arrive on time and be ready to work at the start of their scheduled shift.
  - Employees must notify their manager at least two hours before their shift if they will be late or absent.
  - Unplanned absences should be reported as early as possible using the designated notification process.
  - Excessive tardiness or unexcused absences may result in disciplinary action.


TRACKING
--------
Attendance records are maintained by HR and reviewed periodically. Employees can view their own attendance record upon request.



Acknowledgement: By continuing employment, employees acknowledge
that they have read, understood, and agree to comply with this policy.
