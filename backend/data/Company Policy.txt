OFFICE POLICIES
 
Effective Date: 3/6/2025
Company Name: Hilip Tech

1. WORKING HOURS
- Standard office hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
- Employees are expected to be at their workstations by the start of business hours.
- A one-hour lunch break is provided daily, typically between 1:00 PM and 2:00 PM.
- Flexible working arrangements must be approved in advance by a manager.

2. ATTENDANCE AND LEAVE
- Employees must notify their manager at least 2 hours before their shift if they will be absent or late.
- Paid time off (PTO) requests should be submitted at least 5 business days in advance, except in emergencies.
- Sick leave requires notice as early as possible on the day of absence.
- Unauthorized or excessive absences may result in disciplinary action.

3. DRESS CODE
- Business casual attire is required during regular office days.
- Formal attire is expected during client meetings or official company events.
- Casual Fridays allow neat, presentable casual clothing.

4. WORKPLACE CONDUCT
- All employees must treat colleagues, clients, and visitors with respect and professionalism.
- Harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind will not be tolerated.
- Conflicts should be reported to HR or a direct supervisor promptly.
 
5. USE OF COMPANY PROPERTY
- Company equipment (laptops, phones, tools) must be used primarily for work purposes.
- Employees are responsible for the security and proper care of assigned equipment.
- Any damage or loss must be reported to IT/Admin immediately.

6. INTERNET AND EMAIL USAGE
- Internet access is provided for business-related tasks.
- Limited personal use is permitted as long as it does not interfere with work responsibilities.
- Downloading unauthorized software or visiting unsafe websites is strictly prohibited.
- Company email should be used professionally; confidential information must not be shared externally without authorization.
 
7. CONFIDENTIALITY
- Employees must protect confidential company, client, and employee information.
- Sharing sensitive data with unauthorized parties is grounds for disciplinary action, including termination.
 
8. HEALTH AND SAFETY
- Employees must adhere to workplace safety guidelines at all times.
- Report any hazards, accidents, or unsafe conditions to management immediately.
- Smoking is only permitted in designated areas, if any.
 
9. REMOTE WORK POLICY
- Remote work must be pre-approved by a manager.
- Employees working remotely are expected to remain available during core business hours.
- Data security protocols must be followed when accessing company systems remotely.
 
10. DISCIPLINARY PROCEDURE
- Policy violations will be addressed through a structured process: verbal warning, written warning, final warning, and termination (as applicable).
- Serious misconduct may result in immediate termination.
 
ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
All employees are required to read, understand, and comply with these policies. 
By continuing employment, employees acknowledge acceptance of the terms outlined above.
