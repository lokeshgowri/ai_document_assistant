===============
CODE OF CONDUCT
===============

Company: [Company Name]
Effective Date: [Effective Date]

PURPOSE
-------
This policy establishes the standard of professional and ethical behavior expected of every employee, contractor, and representative of the Company.


SCOPE
-----
This policy applies to all employees, at all levels, across all departments and locations, including remote workers and temporary staff.


POLICY
------
Employees are expected to act with honesty, integrity, and fairness in all business dealings.

  - Treat colleagues, clients, vendors, and the public with respect and courtesy.
  - Comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and Company policies.
  - Avoid conduct that could damage the Company's reputation.
  - Use good judgment and escalate uncertain situations to a manager or HR.
  - Report suspected violations of this Code through the appropriate channel.


COMPLIANCE
----------
Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.



EQUAL OPPORTUNITY AND ANTI-DISCRIMINATION POLICY


Company: [Company Name]
Effective Date: [Effective Date]

PURPOSE
-------
The Company is committed to providing equal employment opportunity to all employees and applicants without regard to any legally protected characteristic.


SCOPE
-----
This policy covers all aspects of employment, including recruitment, hiring, promotion, compensation, training, and termination.


POLICY
------
The Company does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, genetic information, marital status, sexual orientation, gender identity, or any other status protected by applicable law.

  - Employment decisions are based on qualifications, merit, and business need.
  - Reasonable accommodations are provided for qualified individuals with disabilities.
  - All managers are responsible for applying this policy consistently.


REPORTING
---------
Employees who believe they have experienced or witnessed discrimination should report it to HR immediately. All reports will be investigated promptly and confidentially.



Acknowledgement: By continuing employment, employees acknowledge
that they have read, understood, and agree to comply with this policy.
