=======================
DRUG AND ALCOHOL POLICY
=======================

Company: [Company Name]
Effective Date: [Effective Date]

PURPOSE
-------
This policy sets standards for a safe, drug- and alcohol-free workplace.


POLICY
------
  - Employees must not be under the influence of illegal drugs or alcohol while performing work duties.
  - Alcohol may only be consumed at Company-sanctioned events, in moderation, and where legally permitted.
  - Employees taking prescribed medication that may affect job performance should inform their manager or HR.
  - The Company may conduct testing where permitted and required by law or safety-sensitive roles.


SUPPORT
-------
Employees struggling with substance use are encouraged to seek confidential support through the Employee Assistance Program or HR.



Acknowledgement: By continuing employment, employees acknowledge
that they have read, understood, and agree to comply with this policy.

===========================
EMERGENCY PROCEDURES POLICY
===========================

Company: [Company Name]
Effective Date: [Effective Date]

PURPOSE
-------
This policy outlines expected actions in the event of a workplace emergency, such as fire, medical emergency, or natural disaster.


PROCEDURES
----------
  - Familiarize yourself with evacuation routes, assembly points, and emergency exits.
  - In case of fire or evacuation alarm, leave the building calmly via the nearest safe exit and proceed to the designated assembly point.
  - For medical emergencies, contact the on-site first aider or emergency services immediately.
  - Do not use elevators during a fire emergency.


REPORTING
---------
All emergencies and incidents must be reported to Facilities/Security and documented following the event, even if minor.



Acknowledgement: By continuing employment, employees acknowledge
that they have read, understood, and agree to comply with this policy.
